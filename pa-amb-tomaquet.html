Pa amb Tomàquet

Servings: 1

Time: 5 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Rustic or country-style bread
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Tomàquets de ramallet
  • Sea salt

How to Make Pa Amb Tomàquet

Pa amb tomàquet, or "bread with tomato," is one of the most iconic dishes in Catalan cuisine. Simple yet delicious, this traditional recipe dates back centuries and remains a staple in homes, restaurants, and celebrations across Catalonia. It is believed to have originated as a way to soften stale bread, but over time, it has become a beloved side dish or snack that perfectly captures the essence of Mediterranean flavors.

1. Prepare the bread – Use rustic or country-style bread, preferably slightly toasted to add crunch. Cut it into thick slices.

2. Rub the tomato – Take a ripe, juicy tomato, cut it in half, and rub it directly onto the bread until the pulp covers the surface.

3. Drizzle with olive oil – Pour high-quality extra virgin olive oil over the tomato-covered bread for an authentic taste.

4. Season with salt – Sprinkle a pinch of sea salt to enhance the flavors.

5. (Optional) Add extras – Traditionally, you can enjoy it as is or pair it with cured ham, cheese, or anchovies for a more flavorful variation.

Pa amb tomàquet is best served immediately while the bread remains crisp. It is commonly enjoyed as a side dish with grilled meats, charcuterie, or cheese platters. This simple yet flavorful dish represents the Mediterranean diet at its finest—fresh, high-quality ingredients coming together in perfect harmony.
